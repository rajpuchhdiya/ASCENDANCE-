# Training Plan & Loom Videos — Ascendance

Objective: Produce short training videos for editors and administrators. Record with Loom (or similar) and store links in a secure internal doc or private playlist.

Recommended video list (5–12 minutes each):
1. Editor Walkthrough — creating, scheduling, and publishing posts. (5–8m)
2. Media & Image Best Practices — uploading, optimizing, and replacing images. (4–6m)
3. Subscriber Flow — create a test subscriber, check access, renewals. (6–8m)
4. Admin Security — login, 2FA setup, recovery codes, and Wordfence basics. (6–10m)
5. Backup & Restore — how to trigger backups, verify offsite upload, and perform a restore on staging. (6–10m)
6. GDPR & Complianz — how consent works and how to respond to SARs. (6–8m)
7. Deploy & Launch Checklist — how to switch keys and run `scripts/launch_production.php`. (5–8m)

Recording Tips
- Use 1080p, show mouse clicks, and speak clearly. Provide a short summary in the Loom description and a timestamped index for sections.
- Keep credentials out of recordings.

Deliverables
- Loom links for each video stored in the private handover folder or internal wiki.
- A short quiz or checklist for editors to confirm they've watched key videos.
